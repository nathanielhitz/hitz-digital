Clippen – Terms of Service

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Clippen is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Hitz Digital (Netherlands). It turns public Twitch clips into short vertical videos and publishes them to the TikTok and YouTube accounts of the operator. These terms explain the conditions under which the tool is used.

1. Who may use Clippen

Clippen is used only by its operator, on the operator's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it or have content posted on their behalf.

2. What Clippen does

Clippen fetches publicly available clips from Twitch, transcribes them, adds captions, reformats them to a vertical 9:16 video, and uploads the result to the connected TikTok or YouTube account. Each post credits the original Twitch streamer.

3. Content

The clips come from Twitch streamers who take part in public events such as Creator SMP. Streamers who do not want their clips reposted can contact the operator at the address below and the channel is removed from the source list.

4. Platform rules

Clippen operates within the TikTok Terms of Service, the TikTok Community Guidelines, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Twitch Developer Agreement. Content that violates those rules is not published, and the operator removes any published content on request from the platform or the rights holder.
