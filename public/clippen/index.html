Clippen

Turns public Twitch clips into captioned vertical Shorts, automatically.

Clippen is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by Hitz Digital (Netherlands). It is not offered to the public: only the operator uses it, on the operator's own social media accounts. This page explains what the application does and which Google data it uses.

What Clippen does

  • Fetches the most-viewed public clips of the past day from Twitch, for a fixed list of streamers who take part in public events such as Creator SMP.
  • Transcribes each clip and lets an AI model pick the clips worth posting and write a short title.
  • Reformats the clip to a vertical 9:16 video with captions and a credit to the original streamer.
  • Uploads the result to the operator's own YouTube channel as a Short, and prepares the same video for TikTok.

Everything runs unattended on the operator's computer on a daily schedule. Streamers who do not want their clips reposted can ask to be removed from the source list via the contact address below.

How Clippen uses Google data

Clippen signs in to Google with OAuth and asks for one permission only: upload videos to YouTube (the youtube.upload scope). It uses that permission to upload the rendered clips to the operator's YouTube channel, with a title, a description and the tag of the event. Clippen does not read channel data, comments, subscribers or analytics, and it does not access any Google account other than the operator's.

The OAuth token is stored on the operator's computer and nowhere else. No Google user data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.
